SEM–EDX Characterization

SEM–EDX analysis was performed to examine the surface morphology and elemental composition of industrial drainage samples and dried neem leaves. Differences in the elemental composition were observed between the drainage samples associated with plant treatment. These changes suggest that the sample composition was altered following treatment.
The project investigates the potential of plant-based treatment for removing heavy metals, including lead, chromium, cadmium, and nickel, from contaminated water. Confirmation of the removal of these specific metals requires quantitative analysis of their concentrations before and after treatment.
