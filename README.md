# Plant-Based Rapid Water Treatment for Metal Removal

## Project Overview
Metal contamination from industrial and human activities threatens human health and aquatic ecosystems. This project explores a plant-based adsorbent for rapid metal removal from contaminated water, with experimental results demonstrating removal within **1–5 minutes under tested conditions**. A physical PVC pipe prototype has been demonstrated at our institute to illustrate the proposed treatment approach.

## Key Features
- Plant-based adsorbent for metal removal.
- Experimental adsorption data and material characterization.
- Physical PVC pipe prototype demonstration.
- Potential for simple, rapid, and cost-effective water treatment.
